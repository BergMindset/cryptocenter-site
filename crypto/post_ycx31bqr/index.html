Arbitrum TVL Surpasses $3.2 Billion

Arbitrum reached $3.2 billion in TVL with 40% growth over the month

EditorialJuly 9, 20252 min read

The Arbitrum network has demonstrated impressive growth in total value locked (TVL), reaching $3.2 billion in December 2024. This represents a 40% increase over the past month, strengthening the Layer 2 solution's position as the leading Ethereum scaling network.

Key Growth Drivers

The recent TVL growth is driven by increased activity in decentralized exchanges and lending protocols. The perpetual trading platform GMX has grown its TVL to more than $580 million, while Aave's deployment on Arbitrum has attracted over $420 million in deposits.

Key protocols and their metrics:

  • GMX: TVL surpassed $580M
  • Aave on Arbitrum: more than $420M in deposits
  • Camelot DEX: processes over $150M in weekly trading volume
  • RWA protocols: added around $180M in tokenized assets

Migration of Major Protocols

The network has benefited significantly from the migration of several major protocols from Ethereum mainnet. Radiant Capital has deployed substantial liquidity on Arbitrum, while Jones DAO's yield strategies have attracted more than $90 million in assets.

Technical Advantages

Transaction costs on Arbitrum remain consistently low despite the increased activity, with average swap fees below $0.50. This cost efficiency has opened up new use cases, particularly in the algorithmic trading and MEV sectors.

Future Outlook

The upcoming Stylus upgrade promises to expand the network's capabilities by allowing developers to build smart contracts in Rust and C++. This could attract a broader developer base and potentially drive additional TVL growth.

The sustained TVL growth on Arbitrum reflects broader trends in DeFi, where users increasingly favor cost efficiency and transaction speed. As institutional adoption continues, Layer 2 solutions are poised to capture a significant share of the expanding market.
